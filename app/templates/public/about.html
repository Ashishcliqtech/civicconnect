How CivicConnect works

Citizens

Register, report issues with a map pin and photo, track status, receive notifications, and rate the resolution.

Department officers

Review complaints assigned to your department, accept or reject with reasons, add internal notes, and upload proof of work.

Administrators

Manage users and departments, assign complaints, verify resolutions, and monitor analytics and audit logs.

Complaint lifecycle

Submitted → Under Review → Assigned → In Progress → Resolved. Complaints can also be Rejected (with a reason) or Withdrawn by the citizen. Only officers and administrators can resolve a complaint; resolved complaints may be reopened by an administrator.

Your privacy

The public map shows only the category, title, status and an approximate location. Your name, contact details, photos and internal notes are never shown publicly.
